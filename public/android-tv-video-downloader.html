Video Downloader for Android TV

LittleTube is a lightweight video downloader built for Android TV, Google TV and Android TV boxes. It is remote-controlled with a D-pad, has no ads, works on 1–2 GB RAM TVs, and saves downloads directly to the TV gallery (Movies/LittleTube).

Why TV Needs Its Own Downloader

Phone downloader apps are built for touchscreens. On a TV they are awkward: tiny buttons, no remote navigation, heavy interfaces that stutter on low-RAM boxes. LittleTube is built for the TV from the start.

🖥 Remote-First

Every control is D-pad navigable with a big yellow focus state you can see from the couch. No mouse, no keyboard needed.

🪶 Built for Low RAM

Lean interface with no thumbnails, native Media3 playback with hardware acceleration — made for 1–2 GB RAM TVs and boxes.

🚫 No Ads, No Bloat

No ads, no in-app purchases, no proxy servers. Streams play directly over your own connection.

How It Works

  1. Search for a video inside LittleTube, or open it from a link.
  2. Play it with the built-in Media3 player.
  3. Download it to the gallery — video goes to Movies/LittleTube, audio to Music/LittleTube.

Install on Your TV

1 · TV Browser

Open this page in your TV browser, tap Download APK (Direct), open the file, and allow “Install unknown apps” for the browser.

2 · USB Stick

Download the APK on your phone or PC, copy it to a USB stick, plug it into the TV, and open it with the TV file manager.

3 · ADB

From a PC: adb connect <tv-ip>:5555, then adb install app-release.apk.

Devices That Work

Android TV (Sony, Philips, TCL), Google TV, most Android TV boxes, and Fire TV Stick. Samsung Tizen and LG webOS TVs cannot install Android APKs directly — they need a Fire Stick or Android box plugged in (see how to run YouTube on any LED TV).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LittleTube available on Google Play?

No. Like other video downloaders, LittleTube is not on Google Play, because Google does not allow apps that download videos from YouTube. It is installed by sideloading: from the TV browser, a USB stick, or ADB.

Will LittleTube work on my low-RAM TV box?

LittleTube is designed for 1–2 GB RAM TVs and boxes: a lean interface with no thumbnails, native Media3 playback with hardware acceleration, and no background bloat. It is built for the TVs that heavy apps slow down.

Where do LittleTube downloads go?

Videos download directly to the TV gallery, in the Movies/LittleTube folder, and appear in the gallery immediately. Audio downloads go to the Music/LittleTube folder.

Does LittleTube have ads?

No. LittleTube has no ads and no in-app purchases. It is free and open source.

Is LittleTube safe to install?

LittleTube is open source on GitHub, needs no account or sign-up, uses no proxy servers, and every release publishes its SHA-256 hash so you can verify the file you downloaded. Download it only from this site or the official GitHub releases page.

What download quality does LittleTube offer?

LittleTube shows the actual quality of the stream it downloads. It does not upscale video or advertise a quality it cannot deliver.

LittleTube is not distributed through Google Play. Installing it requires allowing “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager — a standard Android permission for apps installed outside the store.

Use the downloader only for content you have the rights to (your own uploads, Creative Commons, public domain). LittleTube is an independent player and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
