Why LittleTube

⬇ Direct Gallery Downloads

Save videos directly to your gallery — the Movies/LittleTube folder — with instant gallery refresh. No file-manager hunting, no extra steps.

🛡 Anti-Crash Engine

Native Media3 playback with hardware acceleration keeps video smooth on 1GB / 2GB RAM Smart TVs that choke on heavy apps.

🌐 100% Serverless

Zero proxy servers. Streams resolve and play directly over your own internet connection — nothing in the middle.

Install in Minutes

1

TV Browser

  1. Open this page in your TV browser.
  2. Tap Download APK (Direct).
  3. Open the downloaded file. When asked, allow “Install unknown apps” for the browser.
2

USB Flash Drive

  1. Download the APK on your phone or PC.
  2. Copy it to a USB stick and plug it into the TV.
  3. Open it with the TV file manager. If blocked, allow “Install unknown apps” for the file manager first.
3

ADB (Developers)

  1. Connect to the TV: adb connect <tv-ip>:5555
  2. Install: adb install littletube.apk

LittleTube is not distributed through Google Play. Installing it requires allowing “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager — a standard Android permission for apps installed outside the store.
