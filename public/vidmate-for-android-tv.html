Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install VidMate on Android TV?

You can sideload the VidMate phone APK on an Android TV, but it is designed for touch phones, not TV remotes. Navigation is awkward and the layout is not made for a TV screen.

Is LiteTube made by VidMate?

No. LiteTube is an independent, open-source app. It is not affiliated with VidMate or VidMate Studio.

Why is LiteTube better for TV?

LiteTube is built for TV from the start: D-pad navigation with a visible focus state, a lean interface for 1–2 GB RAM TVs, no ads, and downloads straight to the TV gallery.
