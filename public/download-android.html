How to install

  1. Tap the download button above to get the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. When asked, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager.
  3. Tap Install, then Open. On a TV, the same steps work from the TV browser — see the YouTube on LED TV guide.

What you get

  • Search, video links and video IDs — play instantly.
  • Direct gallery downloads with instant gallery refresh.
  • Full D-pad navigation for TV remotes, with a large yellow focus state.
  • No thumbnails and native Media3 playback — kind to low-RAM TVs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install the LittleTube APK?

Download the APK, open it, allow “Install unknown apps” for the app you downloaded it from, then tap Install.

Which Android versions does LittleTube support?

Android 5.0 and later, including Android TV and Google TV.

Is LittleTube free?

Yes. LittleTube is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is LittleTube on Google Play?

No. LittleTube is distributed by sideloading from this site, because general YouTube downloaders are not allowed on Google Play.

Where do downloads go?

Videos go to the Movies/LittleTube folder and audio goes to Music/LittleTube, so they appear in your gallery apps.

LittleTube is not distributed through Google Play. Installing it requires allowing “Install unknown apps” for the app you install from — a standard permission for apps installed outside the store.

Use the downloader only for content you have the rights to (your own uploads, Creative Commons, public domain). LittleTube is an independent player and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
