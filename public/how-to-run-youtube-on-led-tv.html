How to Run YouTube on LED TV

You can run YouTube on an LED TV four ways: (1) open the official YouTube app if your TV is a smart, Android or Google TV; (2) cast YouTube from your phone; (3) plug in a Fire TV Stick or Android box if your LED TV is not smart; (4) sideload the lightweight LittleTube app if you want video downloads or your TV is too slow for the official app.

Method 1: Official YouTube App (Smart, Android & Google TV)

This is the simplest method, and the right one for most people.

  1. Press Home on your TV remote and open Apps.
  2. Open YouTube. If it is not installed, open the Play Store on your TV, search for YouTube, and install it.
  3. Open YouTube and sign in if you want your subscriptions and history (optional).
  4. Search for a video and press OK to play.

Method 2: Cast YouTube From Your Phone

If your TV has Chromecast built in, or a Chromecast dongle is attached:

  1. Connect your phone and TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open the YouTube app on your phone and tap the Cast icon.
  3. Choose your TV from the list. The video plays on the TV.

Method 3: Non-Smart LED TV — Add a Fire Stick or Android Box

A non-smart LED TV cannot run apps by itself, but any TV with an HDMI port can be made smart:

  1. Plug a Fire TV Stick, Android box or Chromecast into an HDMI port on your LED TV.
  2. Switch the TV input (Source) to that HDMI port.
  3. Follow the on-screen setup, then open the YouTube app.

See also: LittleTube for Fire TV Stick.

Method 4: LittleTube Sideload (Downloads & Low-Spec TVs)

Use this method if the official app is missing, too heavy for your old TV, or you want to download videos to watch offline:

  1. Open this page (littletube.vercel.app) in your TV browser and tap Download APK (Direct).
  2. Open the downloaded file and allow “Install unknown apps” for the browser when asked.
  3. Open LittleTube, search for a video, and download it to the gallery.

Other install options (USB stick, ADB) are on the Android TV downloader page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every LED TV support YouTube?

No. Only smart TVs have a YouTube app built in — Android TV, Google TV, Samsung Tizen and LG webOS models. A non-smart LED TV cannot run apps on its own; it needs a Fire TV Stick, Android box or Chromecast plugged into its HDMI port.

Can I watch YouTube on a non-smart LED TV?

Yes. Plug a Fire TV Stick, Android box or Chromecast into the TV's HDMI port, switch the TV input to that port, and open the YouTube app on the device. The TV itself only displays the picture.

Why is YouTube slow or crashing on my old TV?

Old TVs often have only 1 GB of RAM and outdated apps. Close background apps and update the YouTube app. If the TV stays slow, a lightweight player such as LittleTube — built for low-RAM TVs with D-pad navigation — runs more smoothly.

Can I download YouTube videos to watch on my TV?

The official YouTube app only offers offline viewing inside the app where supported. LittleTube downloads videos to the TV gallery, in the Movies/LittleTube folder, so you can watch them offline. Only download content you have the rights to.

Is LittleTube the official YouTube app?

No. LittleTube is an independent, open-source player and downloader. It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. For most people the official YouTube app is the simplest choice; LittleTube fits low-spec TVs and users who want downloads.

LittleTube is not distributed through Google Play. Installing it requires allowing “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager — a standard Android permission for apps installed outside the store.

Use the downloader only for content you have the rights to (your own uploads, Creative Commons, public domain). LittleTube is an independent player and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
