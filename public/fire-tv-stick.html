Method 1: Downloader App (No USB Needed)

  1. Install Downloader by AFTVnews from the Amazon Appstore on your Fire Stick.
  2. Open Downloader and enter https://littletube.vercel.app/ as the URL.
  3. Tap Download APK (Direct) on the page, then open the downloaded file.
  4. When asked, allow “Install unknown apps” for Downloader, then tap Install.

Method 2: ADB From a PC

  1. On the Fire Stick, go to Settings and enable ADB debugging.
  2. On a PC on the same Wi-Fi, run: adb connect <stick-ip>:5555 (find the IP under Settings → Network).
  3. Then run: adb install app-release.apk

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install LittleTube on a Fire TV Stick?

Yes. Fire TV is based on Android, so LittleTube installs on it. Use the Downloader app (no USB needed) or ADB from a PC on the same Wi-Fi.

Do I need a USB stick for a Fire TV Stick?

No. The Downloader app method needs only the remote: open Downloader, enter littletube.vercel.app, download the APK and install it. ADB needs a PC instead, not a USB stick.

Will my Fire TV remote work with LittleTube?

Yes. LittleTube is built for D-pad navigation, with a large yellow focus state on every control, so it works fully with the Fire TV remote.

What if my Fire Stick warns about unknown sources?

That warning is normal for apps outside the Amazon Appstore. Allow 'Install unknown apps' only for the app you are installing from (Downloader, or ADB), and download LittleTube only from this site or the official GitHub releases page.

Is LittleTube free on Fire TV Stick?

Yes. LittleTube is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

LittleTube is not distributed through the Amazon Appstore or Google Play. Installing it requires allowing “Install unknown apps” for the app you install from — a standard permission for apps installed outside the store.
