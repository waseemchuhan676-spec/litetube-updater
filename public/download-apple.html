LittleTube for iPhone & iPad

No — LittleTube is not available for iPhone or iPad. LittleTube is an Android app distributed as an APK file, and APK files cannot be installed on iOS. It is also not listed on the App Store.

Why not?

Android and iOS use different app formats. An APK is built for Android only, and iOS devices only install apps from the App Store. Since LittleTube is distributed as an APK from this site, there is nothing an iPhone or iPad can install.

What to do instead

Use LittleTube on an Android phone, tablet or Android TV — the app is free, with no ads. If an iOS version ever becomes available, this page will be updated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a LittleTube app for iPhone?

No. LittleTube is not available for iPhone or iPad.

Why can't I install the APK on my iPhone?

APK files are Android packages. iOS cannot install or run them.

Is LittleTube on the App Store?

No. LittleTube is not listed on the App Store.
